## Reading the matrix

The eight **system roles**, their IZIN, and their PEMBATASAN below are transcribed from the register's slide-04 table. The parenthetical labels belong to the observed role names. **Allowed** means explicitly listed IZIN; **explicitly prohibited / constrained** means the stated PEMBATASAN, including its conditions. **UNSPECIFIED** means no source permission or prohibition was stated for that capability; examples in that column are questions, not permissions or adopted deny-all rules. An unlisted permission is **not granted by this document**, but it is also **not silently converted into a business prohibition**. Decide and source additional permissions before building access rules. `[slide 04]` (IZIN/PEMBATASAN); `[inferred]` (three-state reading and examples).

## Boundaries and decisions still needed

- The *role content* and eight-row count are observed on slide 04, which carries no FR badge. Calling the RBAC concept **FR-2.1** uses an `[assumption]` ID: slide 02c is unavailable; do not cite it as observed or infer its missing text. The BRD §7 and §16.5 preserve that distinction. `[slide 04]` (content); `[assumption]` (FR-2.1 ID).
- The Gudang row states a conditional prohibition on issuing mismatched material, and slide 09 requires rejection of Client A material for a Client B order. The **FR-6.2** numbering under Cutting does not settle whether Gudang, Cutting, or both own the control. This document does not assign module ownership. `[slide 04; slide 09; slide 02]` (behavior); `[inferred]` (document boundary, following register ambiguity).
- Owner's **“Ubah rate card”** and Kepala Produksi/Mandor's **“Tentukan rate card komponen”** are both source permissions. Slide 06 FR-7.2 says “Rate card dikelola Owner.” Who sets, changes, approves, or overrides a component rate and at what point remains undecided; neither permission cancels the other. `[slide 04; slide 06]` (wording); `[inferred]` (unresolved governance question).
- Penjahit reports are verified by mandor; the penjahit restriction concerns **already verified** output. Slide 06 FR-7.3 names mandor as the 30-minute input actor and slide 07's chat transcription says mandor verifies output before payroll. Those statements do **not** create a new automatic payroll privilege for the mandor or a general edit privilege for the penjahit. Finance's listed right is to **calculate** weekly and monthly wages; the source does not specify manual wage adjustments. `[slide 04; slide 06; slide 07 — chat attachment]` (observed actions); `[inferred]` (scope of non-grants).
- QC Inspector may set the slide-04 statuses and record defect types, but no source grants automatic QC override/reopen privileges. Slide 04 says **lolos/reject/repair**, slide 05 badges say **Pass/Repair/Reject**, and slide 08 view 05 says **lolos/repair/scrap**; the register leaves this terminology variance unresolved. `[slide 04; slide 05; slide 08]` (wording); `[inferred]` (no extra grant).
- Slide 05/slide 07 name **Mandor 1, Mandor 2, Kepala Operasional** as bonus recipients, not additional RBAC roles. Their three-way payout (FR-9.2 and FR-10.3 describe one rule) does not grant system access. The BRD §11.1 project-delivery RACI is likewise not a system permission roster. Mapping these people to accounts or the eight roles is **UNSPECIFIED**. `[slide 05; slide 07 — chat attachment; slide 04]` (recipients and roles); `[inferred]` (mapping gap).
- Client portal access is limited to the client's **own `client_id`** data; the source names own consigned stock, order status and real-time progress, not cross-client visibility. Additional portal actions and the precise boundary for internal roles are **UNSPECIFIED**. `[slide 04; slide 02b]` (stated boundary); `[inferred]` (unlisted-action gap).
